## 5. Scene: Accident

The investigation has gone wrong.

{{PLACEMENT}}

The Hazardous-Material Specialist has retreated to high ground. It stands on top of the black equipment case, unmasked and still wearing its yellow vest, feet gripping the lid naturally, looking down at the poop from a safe height.

Its gas mask lies abandoned on the pavement near the poop, where it was dropped. A single loose feather drifts down nearby or lies on the pavement next to the mask.

The Curious Investigator stands in front of the equipment line, its head tilted back and to one side, looking up at its colleague on top of the case, perplexed. Its pose and expression stay natural and pigeon-like, never human.

The poop stays clearly visible in the middle, inside the evidence markers.
