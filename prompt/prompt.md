# Monster Poop — Environmental Incident Investigation

## Image Transformation Prompt

### Objective

Transform a user-provided photograph containing a single piece or cluster of dog poop into a humorous, photorealistic scene depicting two ordinary New York City pigeons investigating the poop as though it were the site of a serious environmental contamination incident.

The image is part of Monster Poop, a public-awareness campaign encouraging dog owners to clean up after their dogs.

The central creative principle is deadpan absurdity: an ordinary piece of dog poop has inexplicably triggered a full-scale environmental investigation conducted by two pigeons wearing miniature hazardous-material safety equipment.

The pigeons take their work very seriously. The photograph should not.

The final result should resemble an authentic street photograph documenting a bizarre real-world event, not an illustration, cartoon, or staged comedy scene.

---

## 1. Preserve the Original Photograph

Preservation of the original photograph is the highest priority.

Treat the uploaded photograph as the source of truth.

* Preserve the original location, architecture, pavement, vegetation, vehicles, street furniture, signage, and background.
* Preserve the original camera angle, perspective, lighting, shadows, depth of field, and photographic characteristics.
* Preserve the original dog poop exactly as photographed, including its size, shape, color, texture, orientation, and location.
* Do not enlarge, replace, reposition, duplicate, or exaggerate the poop.
* Do not beautify, clean up, or otherwise modify the surrounding environment.
* Do not introduce unnecessary changes to the original scene.
* Maintain the original image’s aspect ratio and framing.

Critical constraint: The final image must contain exactly the same number of pieces or clusters of dog poop as the original photograph.

Never invent additional poop, even if additional evidence markers or equipment are present.

The scene must be built around the existing poop, not the other way around.

---

## 2. Overall Scene Composition

Introduce exactly two ordinary, realistically proportioned NYC pigeons investigating the poop.

Position the pigeons on opposite sides of the investigation area, with the original poop clearly visible between them.

The pigeons should be approximately normal pigeon size relative to the pavement and surrounding objects.

The composition should establish two distinct roles:

1. The Curious Investigator: A pigeon standing near the equipment perimeter, studying the poop intently with its head cocked to one side.
2. The Hazardous-Material Specialist: A pigeon wearing a gas mask, positioned closer to the poop and leaning forward to inspect it directly.

### Placement in this photograph

If the photograph contains more than one piece or group of dog poop, the investigation focuses on the most prominent group. Leave any other poop exactly as photographed, outside the investigation area.

{{PLACEMENT}}

Surround the poop with a miniature environmental investigation setup, including evidence markers, scientific instruments, a camera, and containment equipment.

The equipment should be arranged naturally on the pavement, as though the pigeons have been working at the site for some time.

The scene should feel busy enough to suggest a serious investigation, but not so cluttered that the poop or pigeons become difficult to see.

The original poop remains the central subject of the investigation.

---

## 3. Pigeon Appearance — Realistic NYC Street Pigeons

The pigeons must look like authentic feral rock pigeons (Columba livia) commonly encountered on New York City sidewalks.

They should not resemble cartoon birds, domesticated show pigeons, or identical copies of the same bird.

### Natural physical characteristics

* Realistic pigeon anatomy, proportions, and feather structure.
* Naturally rounded bodies with compact heads and short necks.
* Authentic beaks, ceres, eyes, wings, tails, legs, and feet.
* Subtle iridescent green and purple neck feathers.
* Realistic feather layering and variation in texture.
* Natural variations in posture and body proportions.
* Anatomically correct pinkish-red pigeon feet.

Absolutely no human hands, arms, fingers, or human-like gestures.

The pigeons must interact with the scene entirely through their natural body positions, head movements, and gaze.

### Individual variation

The two pigeons should be visibly different individuals.

For example:

Pigeon 1:

* Darker charcoal-gray plumage.
* Subtle checkerboard or speckled wing markings.
* Slightly more compact body.
* Stronger purple-green neck iridescence.
* A slightly smaller or narrower head.

Pigeon 2:

* Lighter slate-gray plumage.
* More pronounced black wing bars.
* Slightly larger or rounder body.
* Different distribution of neck iridescence.
* Subtle differences in beak shape and eye appearance.

These are guidelines rather than rigid character specifications.

Allow natural variation in feather colors, markings, body shape, and proportions between renderings.

The two birds should look like unrelated pigeons encountered on the same NYC sidewalk, not twins.

---

## 4. Feather Condition — Natural, Healthy NYC Pigeons

The pigeons should look like healthy, ordinary city birds photographed outdoors.

Their appearance should be realistic and naturally varied without deliberately introducing dirt, damage, or a weathered aesthetic.

### Natural appearance

* Healthy, intact feathers with authentic layering and texture.
* Subtle natural differences in feather alignment.
* Realistic variations in feather sheen and iridescence.
* Minor asymmetries in plumage, posture, and body proportions.
* Natural differences in feather density and patterning.
* Ordinary feather contours that reflect the birds’ current posture.

The pigeons should not look artificially groomed, but they should not be intentionally made dirty or disheveled.

Do not add grime, dust, scratches, feather damage, or other signs of wear to make the pigeons appear more realistic.

Photographic realism should come from accurate anatomy, natural poses, individual variation, lighting, and texture rather than artificially aging or dirtying the birds.

### Avoid

* Deliberately dirty or muddy feathers.
* Excessively ruffled or ragged plumage.
* Missing feathers or bald patches.
* Visible injuries.
* Artificial scratches or damage.
* Exaggerated signs of urban hardship.
* Unnaturally perfect, identical feather patterns.
* Studio-like symmetry or excessively polished rendering.

The ideal appearance is that of two healthy, ordinary NYC pigeons photographed candidly on a sidewalk.

They should look like real birds, not pristine digital models and not distressed street animals.

## 5. Pigeon 1 — The Curious Investigator (No Gas Mask)

Position the first pigeon at the outer edge of the investigation area, near the equipment case, camera, and evidence markers.

It should remain noticeably farther from the poop than the second pigeon.

### Pose and behavior

* The pigeon stands relatively upright in a natural, relaxed posture, with its body oriented toward the center of the investigation.
* Its feet are planted naturally on the pavement, with its weight distributed slightly unevenly.
* Its neck is gently curved, and its head is cocked noticeably to one side, approximately 25–40 degrees, as though examining something puzzling.
* Its beak points downward toward the poop, and its visible eye is focused intently on the evidence.
* The head tilt should originate from a natural rotation of the neck, not from tilting the entire body.
* Its body remains mostly upright while its head and neck convey curiosity.
* The pose should suggest a pigeon that has noticed something unusual and is trying to understand it.

### Visual character

The pigeon should look intensely curious and slightly perplexed, but without any human-like facial expression.

Its natural eye, beak, and head position should communicate the behavior.

Think of an ordinary NYC pigeon examining an unfamiliar object on the sidewalk, except that this pigeon happens to be wearing miniature hazardous-material safety equipment.

### Spatial relationship

The first pigeon should remain at the equipment line, observing from a safe distance.

The second pigeon, wearing the gas mask, should be positioned substantially closer to the poop, with its head lowered for direct inspection.

This contrast is important: the first pigeon is studying the situation from the perimeter, while the second pigeon is performing the hazardous close-range investigation.

### Avoid

* A perfectly upright, symmetrical, default pigeon pose.
* A straight neck and level head.
* Looking toward the camera or away from the evidence.
* Excessive crouching or bending of the entire body.
* Exaggerated cartoon curiosity or human facial expressions.
* Unnatural neck twisting or anatomically impossible head angles.
* Positioning the first pigeon so close to the poop that its lack of a gas mask becomes visually illogical.

The desired effect is a candid, almost accidental moment of pigeon curiosity, captured in an otherwise absurdly serious environmental investigation.

## 6. Pigeon 2 — The Hazardous-Material Specialist (Gas Mask)

The second pigeon should be positioned significantly closer to the poop, actively examining the hazardous material.

### Pose and behavior

* The pigeon leans forward naturally, lowering its head toward the poop.
* Its body remains anatomically realistic, with its feet planted on the pavement.
* Its head should be close enough to the poop to suggest careful inspection.
* The posture should resemble a real pigeon investigating something on the ground.
* Avoid exaggerated crouching or human-like kneeling.

### Gas mask

The second pigeon must wear a miniature, realistically constructed industrial gas mask.

The mask should:

* Fit naturally around the pigeon’s head and beak.
* Include a clear eyepiece or lens through which at least one eye remains visible.
* Feature a small respirator filter or cartridge.
* Use realistic black rubber, plastic, and metal materials.
* Include believable straps or fastening mechanisms.
* Look like functional protective equipment adapted to pigeon anatomy.

The mask must not create a human-shaped face or distort the pigeon’s anatomy.

The gas mask should feel slightly absurd because of its scale and purpose, but physically plausible within the photograph.

Only the pigeon closest to the poop wears a gas mask.

The other pigeon remains unmasked at the equipment perimeter.

## 7. Protective Equipment

Both pigeons wear miniature yellow hazardous-material safety vests.

The vests should:

* Be bright industrial yellow.
* Feature a clearly visible black radiation hazard symbol.
* Fit naturally around each pigeon’s body.
* Include believable straps, buckles, or fastening points.
* Leave the wings, tail, head, and feet unobstructed.
* Have natural fabric folds and realistic material texture.
* Appear clean, functional, and realistically maintained.

The vests should resemble actual safety equipment rather than costumes.

The birds should still look unmistakably like pigeons.

Do not deliberately add dirt, scratches, scuff marks, stains, fading, or other signs of wear to the vests.

Natural fabric folds and shadows are appropriate, but the uniforms should not be artificially aged or distressed.

Do not add helmets, gloves, boots, or human clothing.

## 8. Environmental Investigation Equipment

Arrange a miniature environmental investigation setup around the original poop.

The equipment should look like realistic professional field equipment scaled appropriately for pigeons.

Include the following elements.

### Numbered evidence markers

Place three small yellow triangular forensic evidence markers labeled:

1, 2, and 3

Arrange them around the original poop in a loose triangular formation.

The markers should be clearly legible, realistically scaled, and naturally positioned on the pavement.

Do not use the markers to imply the existence of additional poop.

### Partial hazard perimeter

Include a short section of black-and-yellow caution tape or striped hazard-boundary material along one side of the evidence-marker triangle.

This is an important visual detail.

The boundary should:

* Connect or visually align with two of the evidence markers.
* Establish a miniature restricted area around the poop.
* Remain low to the ground.
* Look like a small-scale environmental containment boundary.
* Be visible without dominating the composition.

The boundary should not completely surround the scene or obscure the poop.

It should resemble a modest piece of caution tape or striped barrier placed by investigators to mark the hazardous area.

### Black equipment case — Subtle field wear

Include a small, rugged black protective equipment case.

The case should:

* Resemble a professional waterproof field-equipment case.
* Feature a prominent yellow-and-black radiation hazard symbol.
* Have realistic hinges, latches, and a carrying handle.
* Sit naturally on the pavement near the first pigeon.
* Show subtle but visible signs of regular professional field use.

The black equipment case is the only object in the scene that should have intentionally added wear and tear.

Introduce restrained signs of use, such as:

* A few small scratches on the black plastic surface.
* Minor scuff marks along the corners and edges.
* Slight surface wear near the handle or latches.
* Subtle variations in the finish consistent with repeated handling.

The case should look like a well-maintained professional equipment case that has been used repeatedly.

It must not look damaged, neglected, heavily weathered, or excessively dirty.

Keep the radiation hazard symbol clear and legible.

Avoid large patches of missing paint, deep gouges, cracks, heavy dirt, rust, or extensive abrasion.

The wear should be noticeable only upon closer inspection, not a defining visual feature of the scene.

### Camera and tripod

Include a small professional inspection camera mounted on a miniature tripod.

The camera should be positioned near the equipment perimeter and aimed toward the poop.

It should resemble a realistic field-documentation camera rather than a toy.

The camera must be physically coherent: the lens is on the front, and the preview screen is on the back, directly opposite the lens. The lens points at the poop, so the screen faces away from it.

The camera and tripod should appear clean and realistically maintained, without intentionally added wear or damage.

### Environmental detection instrument

Include a compact yellow-and-black handheld radiation or environmental detection meter.

The device should:

* Have a small display.
* Include realistic buttons and controls.
* Sit on the pavement near the evidence area.
* Be appropriately scaled.
* Look like actual professional measurement equipment.
* Appear clean, functional, and realistically maintained.

### Evidence collection bag

Include one clear, empty zip-top plastic evidence bag lying on the pavement near the equipment case.

The bag should have:

* Transparent plastic material.
* Natural wrinkles and reflections.
* A visible resealable closure.
* Realistic contact with the pavement.

The bag must remain empty.

Do not put poop or additional suspicious material inside it.

### Forensic measurement scale

Include a small black-and-white forensic measurement ruler near the original poop.

The ruler should be appropriately scaled and positioned as though documenting the evidence.

It must not obscure or overlap the poop.

## 9. Equipment Placement and Physical Logic

The equipment should appear deliberately arranged by a tiny professional investigation team.

Maintain a believable spatial relationship:

* The black equipment case and camera belong near the first pigeon at the outer equipment line.
* The first pigeon observes the scene from this perimeter.
* The numbered evidence markers define the investigation area around the poop.
* The short caution-tape boundary marks one side of that area.
* The environmental detector sits close enough to the evidence to suggest active monitoring.
* The empty evidence bag lies nearby, ready for collection.
* The forensic ruler sits beside the poop.
* The gas-mask pigeon works inside or immediately beside the marked hazardous area.

The composition should explain visually why one pigeon requires respiratory protection while the other does not.

No pigeon should hold any equipment.

All instruments and tools must rest independently on the ground or on their own stands.

Do not give pigeons hands, arms, or the ability to manipulate tools like humans.

## 10. Photographic Realism

The final image should resemble an authentic photograph taken with a modern smartphone or digital camera.

Match the original photograph’s:

* Lighting direction and intensity.
* Shadow softness and orientation.
* Color temperature.
* Perspective.
* Depth of field.
* Lens characteristics.
* Contrast and exposure.
* Pavement texture and surface detail.

The pigeons and equipment must cast physically believable contact shadows.

Their feet should appear firmly planted on the actual pavement.

Ensure correct scale relationships between the pigeons, poop, evidence markers, and equipment.

Avoid the overly polished, cinematic, or hyper-detailed appearance commonly associated with AI-generated images.

Do not attempt to achieve photographic realism by adding artificial dirt, damage, distress, or weathering to the pigeons or their uniforms.

Realism should come from convincing anatomy, authentic material properties, natural variation, accurate lighting, and seamless integration into the original photograph.

The result should feel like an ordinary street photograph in which something completely ridiculous happens to be occurring.

## 11. Humor and Creative Direction

The humor comes entirely from the contrast between the mundane subject and the seriousness of the response.

An ordinary piece of dog poop is being treated as a major environmental emergency.

The pigeons behave like highly trained specialists conducting a routine investigation.

They do not acknowledge the absurdity of the situation.

The equipment is professional, the procedures appear methodical, and the birds are completely absorbed in their work.

The first pigeon studies the evidence with its head cocked in natural curiosity.

The second pigeon, wearing its gas mask, leans in for a close inspection.

The comedy should be discovered by the viewer, not performed by the pigeons.

Avoid exaggerated expressions, cartoon poses, slapstick, or overt visual jokes.

The image should be funny precisely because everything is presented so seriously.

## 12. Controlled Creative Variation

Maintain a consistent visual identity across the Monster Poop campaign while allowing natural variation between images.

The following elements should remain consistent:

* Exactly two ordinary NYC pigeons.
* Realistic, distinct pigeon appearances.
* Yellow protective vests with radiation symbols.
* One pigeon observing from the equipment perimeter with a curious head tilt.
* One pigeon wearing a gas mask and inspecting the poop up close.
* Three numbered evidence markers.
* A short section of caution tape or striped boundary.
* A black equipment case with a radiation symbol and subtle signs of field use.
* A camera on a tripod.
* An environmental detector.
* An empty evidence bag.
* A forensic measurement ruler.
* Strong photographic realism.
* Deadpan environmental-investigation humor.

Allow variation in:

* Pigeon feather patterns and coloration.
* Body proportions and natural anatomical differences.
* Natural head-tilt angles and neck positions.
* Equipment arrangement.
* Natural vest folds and strap positioning.
* Camera and instrument designs.
* The orientation of the evidence markers.
* The exact placement of the partial hazard boundary.
* The specific location of minor scratches and scuffs on the black equipment case.

Do not introduce creative variation through artificial grunge, damaged uniforms, dirty feathers, or distressed equipment.

The images should feel like different investigations conducted by members of the same tiny environmental response organization.

They should not look like identical scenes copied and pasted onto different sidewalks.

## 13. Important Exclusions

Do not introduce:

* Additional dog poop or duplicate poop.
* Enlarged or exaggerated poop.
* Human hands, arms, or fingers.
* Human-like pigeon anatomy.
* Cartoon pigeons.
* Anthropomorphic facial expressions.
* Perfectly identical pigeons.
* Excessively pristine or symmetrical birds.
* Deliberately dirty or distressed pigeons.
* Injured or abused-looking birds.
* Missing feathers or extensive feather damage.
* Artificial grime or weathering on pigeon bodies.
* Dirty, scratched, faded, or damaged protective vests.
* Intentional wear and tear on equipment other than the black radiation case.
* Excessively damaged or heavily weathered equipment cases.
* Additional animals or investigators.
* Extra gas masks.
* Equipment being held by pigeons.
* Oversized or toy-like scientific instruments.
* Excessive caution tape.
* Full-size human hazard barriers.
* Dramatic smoke, glowing radiation, toxic sludge, or supernatural effects.
* Additional evidence suggesting another source of contamination.
* Unnecessary alterations to the original photograph.
* Advertising slogans, captions, logos, or added typography.

Do not turn the scene into a cartoon, illustration, movie poster, or stylized advertisement.

## 14. Final Composition Priorities

When making creative decisions, prioritize the following in order:

1. Preserve the original photograph and original poop exactly.
2. Never create additional poop.
3. Maintain realistic pigeon anatomy and natural physical variation without artificially adding dirt or distress.
4. Position the curious, unmasked pigeon at the equipment perimeter, with its head noticeably cocked toward the poop.
5. Position the gas-mask pigeon close to the poop, leaning forward to inspect it.
6. Maintain a believable spatial relationship between the pigeons, evidence, and safety equipment.
7. Include the distinctive investigation props, particularly the numbered markers, radiation case, empty evidence bag, and partial caution-tape boundary.
8. Apply subtle, restrained signs of field use exclusively to the black radiation equipment case.
9. Keep the pigeons, protective vests, and remaining equipment clean, natural, and realistically maintained.
10. Match the original lighting, perspective, scale, and photographic characteristics.
11. Preserve the understated, deadpan humor.
12. Allow modest creative variation while maintaining campaign consistency.

## Final Creative Test

The image should make the viewer think:

“Why on earth are two pigeons conducting a hazardous-material investigation of dog poop?”

And then, a moment later:

“Actually, that seems like a reasonable response.”

The pigeons take their work very seriously. The photograph should not.
